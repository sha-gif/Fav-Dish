Sinigang na Baboy

Traditional Filipino Sour Pork Soup

🥬 Ingredients

  1. 1 kilogram pork
  2. 1 packet sinigang mix
  3. 1 onion, sliced
  4. 2 tomatoes, sliced
  5. 1 radish, sliced
  6. 1 eggplant, sliced
  7. 1 bunch kangkong
  8. 5 pieces string beans
  9. 6 cups water
  10. 2 green chili peppers
  11. Fish sauce and salt to taste

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  • Wash the pork and cut it into serving pieces.
  • Put the pork and water in a pot and bring to a boil.
  • Remove the scum from the soup.
  • Add the onion and tomatoes. Simmer until the pork is tender.
  • Add the radish and cook for 5 minutes.
  • Add the eggplant and string beans.
  • Add the sinigang mix and stir well.
  • Add the green chili peppers and kangkong.
  • Season with fish sauce and salt to taste.
  • Simmer for a few more minutes.
  • Serve hot with steamed rice. Enjoy!
